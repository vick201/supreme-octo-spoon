POV Car Racing

Dodge traffic, stay alive, and push your score as high as possible.

Score: 0 Status: Racing

How to Play

  1. Your car is at the bottom of the screen in first-person (POV) view.
  2. NPC cars spawn ahead and drive toward you in lanes.
  3. Move left/right to avoid collisions and survive as long as possible.
  4. Your score increases automatically over time while you stay alive.

Commands

  • ← / A: Move left lane
  • → / D: Move right lane
  • Space: Restart after crash
  • H: Show/Hide this help panel
